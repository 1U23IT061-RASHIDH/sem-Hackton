Welcome to Green Valley Resort

Slow down, breathe deeply and enjoy a welcoming stay close to nature.

Set among leafy gardens, Green Valley Resort offers a relaxed place to reconnect and make time for what matters. Enjoy a refreshing swim, share a meal with family or find a quiet corner to unwind after a day exploring the resort.

Comfortable rooms and welcoming shared spaces make it easy to settle in, whether you are here for a restful weekend, a family break or a special gathering. Discover our rooms and suites and find the stay that suits you.

Why Choose Green Valley Resort?

  1. Unwind in a peaceful setting surrounded by gardens.
  2. Choose comfortable rooms for couples, families and friends.
  3. Enjoy the swimming pool, shared dining and relaxed gathering spaces.
  4. Make time for nature walks and slow, restorative days.

A place to reconnect

Wander through the gardens, enjoy a leisurely meal and take each day at your own pace. Visit our resort gallery to see more of the rooms, pool and garden spaces.

We look forward to welcoming you to Green Valley Resort for a peaceful escape into nature.
